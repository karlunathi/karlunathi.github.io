About Me

I'm an electrical engineer (telecommunications) who found my calling in running operations. For the past three years I've been Training Manager and Operations Manager at a UAV company, where I put the pieces together — people, processes, compliance and customers — so the business runs. When the CEO is away, I step in and lead.

Before UAVs, I started as a marshal at Motorsport Kenya and worked my way up to second-in-command, handling finances and operations. That path taught me to lead under pressure, earn trust at every level, and keep safety and discipline at the centre of everything. I'm now building my skills and certifications to take on bigger responsibility, remotely or on site.
